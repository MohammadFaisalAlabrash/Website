About Me

Analyst. Consultant.
Problem Solver.

“I don’t just configure systems — I redesign the workflows that feed them.”

I am Mohammad Faisal Alabrash — an ERP Consultant and Business Analyst with 5+ years of hands-on experience transforming how real estate and investment organizations operate in Saudi Arabia. My work sits at the intersection of technology and business strategy: I translate complex operational needs into precise, scalable ERP configurations that actually get used.

My expertise covers the full ERP lifecycle — from discovery workshops and Business Requirements Specification (BRS) documentation, through system design, data migration, UAT, go-live, and post-launch optimization. I’ve led a complete multi-module Logix ERP rollout across 6 departments, migrated over 50,000 legacy records with 99%+ accuracy, and trained 40+ staff who adopted the system with minimal friction.

Beyond implementation, I build Power BI dashboards that surface real-time KPIs for leadership, write production-grade SQL reports in SSMS, and automate cross-departmental workflows via Power Automate and SSIS — reducing manual effort by up to 40%. I hold a B.Sc. in Information Technology (First Class Honors, GPA 3.54) from Arab Open University and communicate with equal fluency in Arabic and English.
